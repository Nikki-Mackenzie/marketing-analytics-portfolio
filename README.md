Each project folder contains:
- `README.md`: the case study (question, data, method, findings, recommendations)
- `data/`: raw or sample data, or a note on where to get it
- `sql/` or `notebooks/`: queries and analysis
- `outputs/`: charts, dashboard screenshots and links

## How I work

- **Question first.** Every analysis starts with a decision someone needs to make.
- **Show the reasoning.** Definitions, formulas and assumptions are written down, including where a metric can mislead.
- **Recommend an action.** Findings end with what I would do next and what I would test.
- **Be honest about results.** The real-spend campaign reports what worked and what did not.
